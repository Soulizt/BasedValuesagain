What is BazedValues ALPHA 1?

BazedValues is, in essence, a political quiz for anarchists that attempts to assign percentages for 24 different political values. You will be presented by a statement, and then you will answer with your opinion on the statement, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree, with each answer slightly affecting your scores. At the end of the quiz, your answers will be compared to the maximum possible for each value, thus giving you a percentage. Answer honestly!

There are questions in the test.

What are the 24 values?

There are 12 independent axes, and each has two opposing values assigned to them. They are:

ECONOMISTIC

Those with higher Economistic scores belive that economies are required for a society to function, and that materials have value (either determined either by markets or inherently)

ECONOMY

ANTI-ECONOMY

Those with higher Anti-Economy scores believe that economies have no reason to exist, either due to a lack of belief in property or structured society)

COMMUNALIST

Those with higher Communalist scores are against the ownership of property in the hands of the minority. This generally encompasses private property, however at high scores can involve personal property as well.

PROPERTY

PROPERTARIAN

Those with higher Propertarian scores are in favour of the ownership of pro[erty. They often believe that property is a right to all people, and that theft, trespassing and trademark breaching are crimes.

INDIVIDUAL

Those with higher Individual scores belive that the individual supercedes the community. They value voluntaryism and freedom of choice.

SOCIETY

COMMUNITY

Those with higher Community scores tend to think that the action of the community is far more important that than of the individual. They see that co-operation is required for a society to function.

TRADITION

Those with higher Tradition scores believe in traditional values and strict adherence to a cultural code. Though not always, they are usually religious, and support the status quo or the status quo ante.

CULTURE

PROGRESS

Those with higher Progress scores believe in social change and rationality. Though not always, they are usually secular or atheist, and support full freedom of expression.

PEACEFUL

Those with higher Peaceful scores believe in non-violent methods in establishing and maintaining their desired system, often employing rehabilitative justice. It should be noted that this does not correlate with use of direct action.

AGGRESSION

MILITARISTIC

Those with higher Militarist scores believe in violent/dominant establishment and maintenance of their desired system, generally employing punitive justice systems.

MORALIST

Those with higher Moralist scores believe in ideas such as inherent human rights, objective morality and human-supremacy.

ETHICS

ANTI-HUMANIST

Those with higher Anti-Humanist scores are against the ideas of moralism and humanism, prefering self-derived rights (or no rights at all) and a non-human-centric view. This is not to be confused with misanthropy.

MARKET

Those with higher Market scores belive that a materials value is derived from its scarcity and a person's desire for it, not its uses or requirements. They also tend to believe in either money (o.e) or a bartering system depending on their economic views.

MARKETS

PLANNING

Those with higher Planning scores believe that a material's value comes from its use rather than a person's desire for it. They are against money and bartering, and are in favour of communalisation or equal distribution

TECHNOLOGICAL

Those with higher Technological scores belive that technology should be developed for some benefit at an accelerated rate. Those with paticularly high scores may even be trans or post-humanists.

TECHNOLOGY

PRIMITIVE

Those with higher Primitive scores are against the development of technology. They may believe that humans have progressed too far, and that modern life is unfufilling.

DEMOCRATIC

Those with higher Democratic scores believe that widespread participation is the best way to govern a society.

PARTICIPATION

ANTI-DEMOCRACY

Those with higher Anti-Democratic scores believe that democracy does not act in favour of society for a variety of reasons. This does not neccessarily correlate with hierarchy and oligarchy, however it often does.

INDUSTRY

Those with higher Industry scores believe that industrial expansion and growth is more important than preservation of the environment.

ENVIRONMENT

NATURE

Those with higher Nature scores belive that nature should be protected and taken care of. High scorers could even be considered human extinctionists.

HIERARCHY

Those with higher Hierarchy scores belive that hierarchy is always present in a society, and that this should be embraced. This does not mean that they believe in the existence of a state.

STRUCTURE

HETERARCHY

Those with higher Heterarchy scores believe that hierarchy should be dismantled, due to it not being a requirement for a functional society and its inherent rigidity. Very high scorers could be considered anti-realists, wanting to abolish even the hierarchy of physical constants.

REFORMIST

Those with higher Reformist scores believe in gradual change to establish their ideology. This does not correlate with non-violent tactics.

RADICALITY

REVOLUTIONARY

Those with higher Revolutionary scores believe in rapid change to establish their ideology. This does not correlate with violent tactics.


What's the "Closest Match" mean at the bottom of the results?

In addition to matching you to the 24 values, the quiz also attempts to match you to a political ideology. This is a work in progress and is much less accurate than the values and axes, so don't take it too seriously. If you disagree with your assigned ideology, send us an email at rollingpandas@outlook.com with your scores, matched ideology, and preferred ideology, and we'll look into adjusting the system.
